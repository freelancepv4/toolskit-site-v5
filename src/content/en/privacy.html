Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to your files, your inputs and your data when you use ToolsKit. Last updated {{updated}}.

Summary

  • ToolsKit tools run in your browser. Files you add and text you type are processed on your device and are not uploaded to ToolsKit.
  • ToolsKit sets no cookies, shows no ads and currently uses no analytics.
  • Your browser stores a short list of recently opened tool names, your language choice if you pick one, and a CV draft if you choose Save draft. All of it stays on your device.
  • The website’s hosting provider handles ordinary request logs, as any web host does.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Your files and inputs

PDF, image, QR code, converter, calculator, CV and social media tools do their work with JavaScript inside your browser tab. The files you choose are read by your browser and the results are created on your device; the download is produced by your browser. No ToolsKit code sends your files or the text you type to ToolsKit or to anyone else, so ToolsKit cannot see them, keep them or recover them.

Because processing happens on your device, performance depends on your device’s memory, and a tool can fail on very large files. Nothing is saved after you close or reload the page, except the items listed in the next section.

What your browser stores

  • Recently used tools — the names (IDs) of up to six tools you opened, in your browser’s local storage, so the All tools page can list them. You can clear the list on the All tools page or by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • Language choice — if you pick a language from the language list, its code (for example “de”) is kept in your browser’s local storage so the home page can list that language first next time. It is never used to redirect you and is never sent anywhere.
  • CV draft — only if you choose Save draft in the CV & Resume Builder. It stays in your browser’s local storage until you choose Clear draft or clear site data. On a shared computer, clear it before you leave.

ToolsKit’s own code does not set cookies.

Analytics and advertising

ToolsKit does not currently use analytics or advertising, and loads no tracking scripts. The site’s code includes an optional, consent-based measurement layer that is switched off. If analytics or advertising is ever enabled, this policy will be updated first, consent will be requested where the law requires it, and file contents, typed inputs and search text will not be collected.

The site does not use third-party fonts, social media widgets or embedded third-party content. Pages and libraries are delivered by a web hosting provider, which is a third-party service (see the next section). Links on this site that start with “mailto:” open your own email program.

Server logs

When your browser requests a page, the hosting provider receives technical information such as your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser and device type. Hosts typically use this to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose problems, and keep it for a limited period under their own policies. This is request information only: ToolsKit tools do not send your files or inputs in these requests. The hosting provider may process this data in different countries.

If you contact us

If you send an email, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, to investigate a reported problem and to keep a reasonable record of the conversation. We delete it when it is no longer needed, and sooner if you ask. Please do not send private files, passwords or financial details.

Retention

ToolsKit holds no copies of your files or inputs. Items in your browser’s local storage remain until you clear them. Emails are kept as described above. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider for its own retention period.

Security

Processing in your browser means your files do not travel to a ToolsKit server. ToolsKit is intended to be served over HTTPS and sends security headers that restrict the page to loading code from its own site. No system is perfectly secure: keep your browser up to date and be careful on shared computers. Note that a Wi-Fi QR code contains the network password in readable form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy, and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data ToolsKit itself is likely to hold is an email you have sent us, so those requests mean finding and deleting that correspondence. Use the Contact page to make a request. Data held by the hosting provider is subject to that provider’s own policy.

Children

ToolsKit is a general-purpose utility site and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when ToolsKit’s practices change, for example if analytics or advertising is added, and change the “last updated” date above. Material changes will be described here before they take effect.
