Why ToolsKit

  • Straight to the tool

    Each task has its own page, with the tool at the top.

  • Simple

    Clear interfaces for everyday tasks, with limits stated plainly.

  • Runs in your browser

    Files and inputs are processed on your device and are not uploaded to ToolsKit.

  • No account

    None of the current tools asks you to sign up, and all of them are free.

Files you add are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to ToolsKit. The PDF and QR libraries are served from ToolsKit’s own site, not from a third-party CDN.
