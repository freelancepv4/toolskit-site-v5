About ToolsKit

ToolsKit is a collection of free online tools for everyday tasks: working with PDFs and images, creating QR codes, converting units, doing quick calculations, building a CV and drafting social media text.

Who it is for

ToolsKit is designed for students, professionals, creators, small businesses and everyday users who need a quick browser-based utility — compressing a PDF before emailing it, resizing a photo for a form, working out VAT on an invoice, or making a QR code for a Wi-Fi network — without installing software or creating an account.

Principles

  • Runs in your browser. Every current tool does its work on your device. Files you add and text you type are not uploaded to ToolsKit.
  • No account. None of the tools asks you to sign up, and no accounts are offered.
  • Free. Every current tool is free to use.
  • Honest limits. Each tool page says what the tool does not do. When no tool matches a search, ToolsKit says so instead of showing something unrelated.

ToolsKit remembers the tools you recently opened (tool names only) on your own device, and the CV builder saves a draft on your device only if you choose Save draft. ToolsKit sets no cookies and currently uses no analytics or advertising. Like any website, the hosting provider handles ordinary request logs. The Privacy Policy has the full detail.

Limits

  • Calculators give estimates from the numbers you enter. They are not financial or tax advice, and the VAT calculator does not look up country rates.
  • Compress PDF re-saves a file more efficiently but does not recompress images, so photo-heavy PDFs may barely shrink.
  • ToolsKit does not currently convert PDFs to Word or Excel, edit PDF text, sign, unlock or OCR PDFs.
  • Caption, hashtag, title, description and bio generators use templates, not AI, and do not guarantee performance.
  • Single PDF or image files over 150 MB are rejected in the PDF tools, and very large files can fail on devices with little memory.
  • ToolsKit is available in English. A growing selection of tools is also written for German, French, Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Polish; those pages were written for each language rather than copied from English, but they have not yet been reviewed by professional editors. Please report mistakes on the Contact page.

How the tools are maintained

ToolsKit is a small project. Each tool is a self-contained page that runs in your browser, and the whole site is generated from one shared set of tool data and templates so that names, navigation, privacy wording and links stay consistent. New tools are added when they are genuinely useful and can run in the browser. Bugs, questions and ideas are welcome on the Contact page.
