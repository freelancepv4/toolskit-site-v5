ToolsKit serves these open-source libraries from its own origin (`/assets/vendor/`). No third-party CDN is used at runtime.

| Library | Version | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| pdf-lib | 1.17.1 | MIT | Merge, split, rotate, compress, images to PDF |
| PDF.js (+ worker) | 3.11.174 | Apache-2.0 | PDF to images |
| JSZip | 3.10.1 | MIT or GPL-3.0 | ZIP output for multi-image downloads |
| qrcodejs | 1.0.0 | MIT | QR code drawing |

Licence texts are in `docs/licenses/`. Confirm versions and licence text against the files in `src/assets/vendor/` before launch.
